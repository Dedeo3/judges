External dApp

A separate site, on its own origin, using @judges/sdk installed from the published package — not part of the Judges app. Clicking verify opens a Judges popup; the passkey ceremony runs there, and only the proof comes back here.

Your contract must be deployed with domain = hashToField(…) — the app id is namespaced under this site's origin, so no other site can spend your users' turns.
